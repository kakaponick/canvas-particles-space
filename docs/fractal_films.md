# Fractal films

The rules for the code of the Mandelbrot zoom: fractal films and the page's Mandelbrot mode. `docs/films.md` holds the film machinery they share, and `CLAUDE.md` the words *gated* and *deterministic* used here.

A fractal film is the film renderer forked for another picture: a zoom into the Mandelbrot set, to any depth, described by one JSON object whose format is `FRACTAL_GUIDE` in `src/lib/fractal.ts` (`window.space.fractal.guide()`, the `space_fractal_guide` tool). It touches none of the scene and none of the per-frame path. Visitors see the same zoom live in the page's Mandelbrot mode.

- **A fractal film is a timeline like a film.** `tracks` move the values in `FRACTAL_TRACKS` along keyframes; `rate`, `spin` and `drift` are paces, so what a frame shows is their sum (`sumTrack`, summed `SUM_RATE` times a second): a rate that slows to 0 holds the camera where it is, one below 0 pulls it back, and the depth and the bits follow from the sum. `fade`, `captions` and `shots` are read by the particle film's own readers (`readPace`, `readCaptions` with a `CaptionScope` of the fractal's places and numbers, `readShots`) and drawn by its captions (`CaptionPlan`, `CaptionFrame`); a caption's `#{key}` reads `FRACTAL_NUMBERS`. A film without tracks takes the formula it always took.
- **A fractal script is the film's language.** `playScript` in `film_script.ts` plays a script and a kind (`ScriptKind`) says what it writes: `writeFilm` a film, `writeFractal` a fractal film, whose words are the film's but those of the particle scene (`FRACTAL_SCRIPT_WORDS`) and whose values are its tracks and fade. A new fractal value goes into `FRACTAL_TRACKS` and both the JSON and the script take it; the audit holds that the guides tell every key, word and number and that a script and its JSON give one plan.
- **It shares the film's machinery and none of the scene.** `encodeVideo`, `exclusively`, the progress, the cancel and the contact sheet (`framesAt`, `sheetLayout`, `createSheet`) are in `film_render.ts` and serve both renderers; the readers, captions and script engine above are the film's too, and a reel plays a fractal clip through the fractal's own player.
- **The target is exact, the pixels are not.** `fractal_orbit.ts` places a Misiurewicz point by Newton's method on BigInt fixed-point numbers, to the bits the deepest frame needs plus `GUARD_BITS`. Only the target's orbit reaches the GPU, as float32 pairs in a texture: a Misiurewicz point's preperiod and one turn of its cycle, which the shader loops over. Any other point is stored as its BigInt orbit up to where it escapes.
- **A pixel is perturbation with an exponent of its own** (`fractal_shaders.ts`, GLSL ES 3.00): δ and dz/dc are a mantissa and an exponent, the step at the orbit's first entry (where Z is 0) gives each term its own exponent, and a pixel whose orbit comes nearer 0 than the reference, or reaches the end of an ending orbit, is rebased to the first entry. That is what keeps one reference free of glitches at any depth.
- **A frame is a function of its second.** `fractalView` gives the pixel size, the turn, the colours' phase and the steps a pixel takes (`stepsToReach` on the orbit's growth of |dz/dc|), so a preview draws only its frames. A loop zooms whole copies of the picture (the cycle's multiplier), turns back by their angle, shifts the palette by their steps and keeps one step budget, so its last frame leads into its first.
- **A frame is drawn in bands of rows,** each waited for with a fence and sized to about `BAND_MS`, so a heavy frame never holds the GPU long enough for the system to reset it.
- **The page's Mandelbrot mode costs the scene nothing.** Its button sits in the scene toolbar and its panel follows the UX rules of the others (`fractal_mode.tsx`); its state is `fractal_store.ts`, its target and palette names are `fractal_catalog.ts`, the one fractal module the page loads with itself. While it is open the particle scene is paused through `isPaused`, so `syncLoop` stops its loop, and Settings and the intro debugger, which belong to the scene, close it and are closed by it. The live view (`fractal_live.ts`) and the renderer are loaded when the mode is first opened. It runs a loop of its own, sends each frame in one pass (`drawNow`) and holds its frame time by the frame's size, a share of the screen's pixels that shrinks when frames come late; a paused view is drawn again at full size with 2×2 samples, in bands, and the loop then draws nothing. Its target never needs more than a double's bits: a Misiurewicz orbit is found once at `LIVE_BITS` and the depth is a number. Save MP4 renders the fall from where the camera is with the film renderer (`liveFilm`, `renderFractal`) and hands the file to the browser's download.

## Verify

A fractal change is done when each check that applies passes:

- Two previews of one fractal film are the same bytes, and earlier fractal films keep theirs or change as meant.
- A change to the perturbation: a point target and the Misiurewicz target at the same place draw the same frames.
- A change to what it shares with particle films (see `docs/films.md`): particle and fractal films keep their bytes or change as meant, and a fractal film still renders.
