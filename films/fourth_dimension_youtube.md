```

From a point to a line, a square, a cube and a tesseract (a hypercube), the film builds the rule of dimensions, then takes it to a black hole, the textbook gravity well, gravitational waves and a neutron star, and to an experiment that could test it: an atomic ruler as long as light travels in one second.
```
